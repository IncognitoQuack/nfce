- **Not investment advice.** This is research and educational software. No version showed an edge after costs. It places no orders.
- **Third-party data.** This package contains no market data except `evidence/tv_v2_base_trades.txt`, the strategy's own trade log, derived from TradingView charts.

- **Secrets.** No secrets are included. `nfce_bridge/.env` is excluded; `.env.example` is an empty template.
